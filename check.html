Batch Scheduling Windows

Processing runs at two rates depending on when a batch is submitted. Standard rate applies across most of the week; an elevated rate applies during two fixed weekday windows. Batches submitted at any time complete in full — only the rate changes.

Best time to submit

Any time Saturday or Sunday — 48 continuous hours at standard rate.

Longest weekday window

15:30 onward, giving 8½ hours at standard rate every weekday evening.

Mid-morning gap

09:30–11:30 sits between the two elevated windows and bills at standard rate.

Weekly split

133 of 168 hours — 79% of the week — are at standard rate.

All times Indian Standard Time (UTC +5:30). Elevated windows apply Monday to Friday only; weekends carry no elevated period. Batches submitted during an elevated window still process to completion — the rate is the only difference.
